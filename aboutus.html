About Us

Barnes & Noble is the nation’s largest retail bookseller, with a rich history dating back to 1886. Our mission is to provide readers with access to books, knowledge, and an immersive literary experience both in-store and online.

Our Legacy & Growth

Founded in 1886 by Charles M. Barnes, Barnes & Noble has evolved from a small bookshop in Wheaton, Illinois, to a nationwide bookstore chain with over 600 locations across the United States. Through decades of growth and innovation, we have remained a trusted source for book lovers and literary enthusiasts.

Visit Us or Shop Online

Explore our wide selection of books, gifts, and more at a store near you or visit our online store at BarnesandNoble.com. Whether you're looking for the latest bestseller, a classic novel, or a thoughtful gift, Barnes & Noble is here to help you find what you love.

Commitment to Literacy & Education

At Barnes & Noble, we believe in the power of books to transform lives. Through our literacy initiatives, we support schools, libraries, and reading programs to foster a love for books in people of all ages. Our partnerships with educators and organizations help make books more accessible to underprivileged communities.

The Barnes & Noble Experience

We are more than just a bookstore. Each of our locations offers a welcoming environment where readers can discover new books, enjoy a cup of coffee at our in-store cafés, and attend book signings, readings, and community events. Our knowledgeable booksellers are always ready to help customers find their next great read.

Sustainability & Responsibility

We are dedicated to sustainability and environmentally responsible practices. Our efforts include reducing our carbon footprint, using sustainable materials in book production, and promoting eco-friendly shopping bags and packaging. We continually seek ways to minimize waste and contribute positively to the environment.

Partnerships & Community Engagement

Our partnerships with authors, publishers, and educational institutions enable us to support the literary world in meaningful ways. We also work closely with local communities, hosting events that celebrate books and storytelling while providing a platform for emerging and established writers.
